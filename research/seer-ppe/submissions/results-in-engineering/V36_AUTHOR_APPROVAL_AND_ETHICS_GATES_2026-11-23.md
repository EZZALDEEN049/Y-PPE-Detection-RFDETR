- Identify the **actual** applicable committee/institution, approval decision, waiver or reason a documented exemption is valid for retrospective engineering field images. If an ethics-board decision is required, provide the actual dated evidence; this editorial team cannot infer or generate one.

- Confirm whether any raw worker faces, site signage, embedded location/time metadata, filenames, or other personally identifiable information appear in planned public releases.

- Confirm institutional and organizational permission for use of images, if required under applicable rules. The original research dataset and protected Test split remain unchanged.
