# SEER-PPE — Higher-acceptance, reputable journal evidence screening

**Checked on:** 23 November 2026  
**Manuscript:** SEER-PPE v0.43 main + v0.43 Supplementary Material.  
**Objective:** Search for publisher-reported high journal acceptance rates in relevant computer vision, imaging, and applied engineering journals without inventing manuscript acceptance probabilities.

## Main findings

**NO CURRENT, OFFICIALLY VERIFIED 70–90% journal-wide acceptance rate** was found among reputable, on-topic Elsevier / Springer Nature computer-vision and engineering journals in this screening. Claims of 60–90% should be rejected unless accompanied by a current, unambiguous journal-level primary source. Acceptances of *individual special issues*, *reviewer invitations*, and *model-generated predicted user acceptance* are not journal acceptance rates. Historical metrics are not current probabilities.

**Best historically high, editorially relevant figure**: *Scientific Reports*, Springer Nature / Nature Portfolio — its **7 July 2022 editorial board guide** states **approximately 50% overall acceptance**. This is *historic, not confirmed for 2025/2026*. The journal deliberately considers technically valid, original studies and negative results in all engineering disciplines. Publisher lists **2025 JIF 4.9**, current engineering remit. Gold OA; corresponding-author low-income country APC waiver may apply in Yemen according to Springer Nature policy, **subject to confirmation**.

**Best current, publisher-disclosed engineering-application rate**: *Engineering Reports* (Wiley) — **34% acceptance rate**, **2025 Journal Impact Factor 2.9**, broad engineering and computer science focus. It could accept a well-framed diagnostic object detection / black-box perturbation study with supported conclusions, not generalized safety claims.

**Best current, publisher-disclosed imaging rate**: *IET Image Processing* (Wiley/IET) — **33% acceptance rate**, **2025 Journal Impact Factor 2.4**; imaging/image analysis scope matches saliency-grid robustness. More methods/validation focus required.

**Additional actual current publisher rate**: *PLOS ONE* — **26% for calendar-year 2025** from PLOS's official journal metrics (not the historical ~48-50% from 2020–2021). It expressly considers technically rigorous negative and replication results; however, strong ethics and data/code availability policies could materially affect a worker-image corpus. PLOS offers *zero APC for authors at Research4Life Group A institutions with no external research funding*, claimed at submission, subject to eligibility.

**Known lower candidate**: *IEEE Access* reports ~**20%** overall average acceptance, so it is not a high-acceptance strategy despite other strengths.

### False positives screened out
- *Discover Internet of Things* has an editorial article published in 2026 describing only **14–20% acceptance**, despite broad remit; no high-acceptance claim is appropriate.
- A Taylor & Francis *Journal of Applied Non-Classical Logics* page reports **50%** acceptance, but it is **out of scope** (formal and philosophical logic), hence must not be recommended for PPE/XAI merely to optimize percentage.
- The Wiley *Plant Phenome Journal* lists **64%** but is plant phenotyping, **out of scope** for worker PPE.
- *Expert Systems* (Wiley) 3% and *IET Computer Vision* (Wiley) 13% are not easy-entry venues, despite topical relevance.
- The 2017 scholarly article about open-access mega-journals discusses much older 55–69% rates. Those are historic and cannot be reused as 2026 facts.
- A 2026 Pattern Recognition special issue reported 34% selection, **not** the journal-wide acceptance rate.

## Recommended sequence aligned to user's goal

**If the author wants a reputable, relatively approachable outlet rather than highest IF:**
1. **Scientific Reports** — *most promising combination of historical ~50% soundness criterion and breadth*, but **must reconfirm current selectivity** and ensure journal ethics/data sharing requirements. Do not suggest it is 50% likely to accept SEER.
2. **Engineering Reports** — **34%** current publisher rate; reasonable broad applied engineering framing and a journal-backed rate.
3. **IET Image Processing** — **33%** current publisher rate and excellent topic fit; consider if imaging discipline matters more than broad engineering.
4. **PLOS ONE** — **26% (2025)**, transparent soundness standards and potential full funding waiver for qualifying Yemen-affiliated authors; strong data/ethics conditions.
5. If Elsevier-only, **Results in Engineering** remains a journal-fit candidate but **its acceptance rate could not be validated**. The difference between broad scope and an actually high acceptance rate must not be blurred.

**Required before submitting to *any* journal:** complete credible worker-photo privacy/consent/ethics documentation, author funding/competing interests/contributions, prior-publication differentiation, clear explanation that model evaluated on 459 Validation images, but perturbation on only ten selected targets from eight development-exposed images, correct uncertain generalization/comparator claims, article compression of the 62-page main manuscript, accurate data/software availability statement. There has been no new model evaluation, Test access, or actual submission in this review.
