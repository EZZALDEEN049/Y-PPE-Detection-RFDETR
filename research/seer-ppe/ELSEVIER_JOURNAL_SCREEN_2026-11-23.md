# SEER-PPE — Elsevier journal selection memo

**Screened:** 23 November 2026  
**Based on:** SEER-PPE research manuscript v0.43 (62-page main + 34-page supplement).  
**Status:** Publisher and secondary academic indexing research; no journal submission or acceptance prediction.

## Decision

- **Strongest disciplinary match and 2025 SCIE JCR Q1:** **Image and Vision Computing**. Elsevier advertises JIF **5.0**; scope includes image interpretation, real-world vision, quantitative method comparison and performance evaluation. Editorial competitiveness is **high**; the current frozen-backbone, ten selected target/eight-image XAI case study needs sharper incremental novelty, sensitivity controls and a considerably shorter primary narrative.
- **Best potentially realistic Q1 + APC-free-for-eligible-authors choice:** **Results in Engineering**. Elsevier's broad interdisciplinary engineering Gold Open Access journal; publisher's indexed ScienceDirect call-for-papers list reports JIF **9.4**. Comillas Pontifical University IIT reports **2025 JCR Q1 and SJR Q1**; classification listed in secondary data as **ESCI rather than SCIE**. For all authors affiliated in Research4Life Group A states, Elsevier confirms 100% APC waiver for fully OA titles; Yemen is a Group A country. Verify the author-team affiliations and the university's acceptance of ESCI vs SCIE before using this as a Q1 requirement.
- **Third Q1 SCIE option:** **Computers & Electrical Engineering**, Elsevier JIF **5.5**, university-indexed 2025 JCR Q1/SJR Q1; scope includes computational techniques, AI methods/applications, signal processing and information systems. Fit is **moderate** and stronger for a substantial computer-engineering method than the current report of fixed-detector saliency instability.
- **Subject-specialized Q2 SCIE alternative:** **Computer Vision and Image Understanding**, Elsevier JIF **3.6**, secondary 2025 JCR Q2. The publisher explicitly covers image understanding, recognition and interpretation. Requires stronger broad methodological insight.
- **Broader visual processing backup:** **Journal of Visual Communication and Image Representation**, Elsevier JIF **2.8**, exact 2025 JCR quartile **not verified here**. Official scope covers image analysis, computer vision, visual representation and deep learning for visual signals. No guaranteed indexing quartile stated.
- **High-selectivity stretch:** **Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence**, JIF **9.0**, real-world novel AI and replication on public datasets explicitly expected; publisher warns desk-rejection if abstract does not separate AI contribution from engineering application, undefined acronyms, or missing single-column format. This study as executed is **not** a first-submission candidate without additional experiments and data-rights plans.
- **Construction field outcome stretch:** **Automation in Construction**, stronger occupational/construction evaluation would be expected; this study currently lacks tested worker-level compliance or on-site safety effects.

## Fee/waiver policy (official Elsevier 2026)

Elsevier automatically grants 100% APC waivers for **fully open access** articles only when corresponding and co-authors' organizations are **all in Research4Life Group A countries**. Mix with other Research4Life Group B countries usually changes to 50%; inclusion of noneligible countries can remove the Research4Life waiver. It cannot be inferred from the lead author's nationality alone. Verify Research4Life current Yemen categorization and all affiliations in the publishing system.

Hybrid journals' **subscription model** ordinarily has **no APC**; choosing optional open access in a hybrid journal is not covered by the same automatic waiver.

The standard DOAJ-listed Results in Engineering APC of about USD 2,160 and other independent price snapshots vary; **do not treat the historical price as a live quote**. Final price/waiver is determined by the publisher upon acceptance and payment selection.

## Best candidate strategy

**Path A — journal-neutral current completed evidence, no new model inference:**
1. Present a diagnosis of *target-matched black-box occlusion and cross-grid saliency rank stability* for a frozen 17-class RF-DETR instance detector, not a novel detector architecture and not worker-level compliance.
2. Condense 62-page main document into a focused article. Editor-set planning target **~6–8k words only**; this is **not** an Elsevier journal's verified limit. Shift case histories, audit ledgers and many of the 16 tables to supplements.
3. Clearly describe 459 original validation images and 10 XAI targets/8 development-exposed images; include observed negative findings and lack of A5/A6 superiority, deterministic random baseline, source-dependence and single-resolution exceptions.
4. Complete actual ethics/consent/worker-image privacy legal basis, funding/COI/contributions and sharing policy, and distinguish all prior 2025/2026 Y-PPE publications. Do not invent approvals.
5. If a **high-quality SCIE Q1 specialist journal is non-negotiable**, prioritize Image and Vision Computing **only after** significant robustness/statistical strengthening. If **ESCI JCR Q1 acceptable** and no-fee Gold OA needed, target Results in Engineering first. If the institution requires SCIE and no new experiment, consider C&EE only if technical computer-engineering claim can withstand peer review; otherwise choose an appropriate lower-tier outlet instead of overstating novelty.

**Path B — competitive SCIE Q1 vision paper (additional future work):**
- Independent source/site validation, enough independently sampled XAI targets, repeated random baselines and uncertainty summaries by image/site, stronger quantitative comparator and nonduplication of prior author's XAI/architecture publications.
- Direct hardware latency/energy only if such claims are essential.
- No new model calls/retraining/split change/Test access was approved or performed in this screening.
