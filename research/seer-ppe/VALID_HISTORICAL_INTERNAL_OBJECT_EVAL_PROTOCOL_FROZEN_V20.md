## Prediction-to-image identity join

For each historical Eval Valid row, inspect its **documented returned image identity field**. Join exactly once to original Valid source manifest (`SOURCE_ID_MANIFEST_TRAIN_VALID_2026-11-19_V01.json`) by a **unique original full filename** or a uniquely matched deterministic Roboflow export suffix convention. Do **not** use fuzzy semantic matching, same basename when duplicate, similarity rank, or confidence to resolve identity. Source-ID and split counts, the 459 vs 468 boundary and the 9 exclusions must reconcile. If the saved Eval's input images may have changed due to preprocessing/augmentation, do not assert pixel-level correspondence merely from filenames.

## Locked metrics if source-to-eval matching succeeds

- Primary **descriptive endpoint:** aggregate **micro precision, recall and F1** from Roboflow Eval's stored **per-image TP, FP, FN** at the fixed platform confidence threshold **0.20** and the platform's documented original evaluation matching definition, if verifiable. Summation is performed *after* exact cohort filtering, separately for M0/M1/M2: P=TP/(TP+FP), R=TP/(TP+FN), F1=2TP/(2TP+FP+FN). Undefined denominators return null, **never zero**.
- **Secondary:** source-size and class-error breakdown only where fields are complete and semantics are verified; image-level false positive, false negative, and empty-annotation counts. Keep original class categories, including negative-labelled PPE classes, as detection classes only.
- **No AP from aggregate TP/FP/FN.** Historical platform-reported mAP may be described separately with attribution and threshold/protocol if available, but must not be relabeled as newly computed 459/M1/M2 mAP. Never compute worker PPE compliance/violation or claim SEER safety improvement from original object detection metrics.
- **Confidence intervals:** no acquisition-independent or clustered inferential confidence interval until independently evidenced groups are known. Observed micro values may be presented without inferential uncertainty or with clearly identified ungrouped image-bootstrap only as an exploratory sensitivity, never as family-independent generalization.
- **Effect sizes:** changing from M0 to M1/M2 yields **descriptive coverage/micro-metric shifts** (different samples). It does not show improved model performance, independent generalization or prove leakage removal.
