# SEER-PPE V24 — targeted verified related-work collision screening, 2024–2026

**Date:** 2026-11-21. **Scope:** targeted primary-source and journal-abstract checks relevant to the specific novelty claims in the working master's research manuscript. This is **NOT** a systematic review, a proof that no closer study exists, or an executed SEER-PPE comparative result. The original researcher's historical publications remain separately cited as prior work. Do not change original model, images, labels, splits or use Test.

## Why this review is necessary

The work previously contained the statement that a "complete 2024–2026 bibliographic collision matrix" was pending. New targeted checking found **specific, named and attributable prior works** on several purported ingredients—RF-DETR, selective explanation quality/cost, context-aware PPE interpretation, regulation-aligned visual relationships, PPE explanation consistency and uncertainty-gated heavy verification. Thus SEER-PPE cannot responsibly claim invention or worldwide priority in any of those already studied ingredients. Its *candidate distinction*, if empirically demonstrated, must be **joint safety-constrained selective explanation with target-matched fidelity and measured incremental computational/review cost on the same fixed PPE detector** and honest abstention when individual task rules are unknown.

## Provisional, non-overclaiming formulation of the SEER research hypothesis

**Hypothesis to TEST, not a result:** Given the **same fixed original detector and source images**, a safety-constrained policy that (a) separates observed PPE cues from worker-specific PPE states and task mandates, (b) abstains from safe clearance on missing worker coverage or source ambiguity, and (c) selectively requests target-matched *fidelity-evaluated* explanations or actual human review, may achieve lower incremental explanation computation than a strong uncertainty-gated and review/compute-matched alternative without violating prespecified worker-risk constraints.

**Acceptable potential contribution** (subject to real experimental evidence):
1. **Joint evaluation contract**, not invention of detection, selective explanations, PPE relationships, context rules or abstention themselves.
2. **Instance-specific explanation fidelity controls** for existing RF-DETR object predictions (matched target, deletion/insertion with LOST/AMBIGUOUS recorded), which are more relevant than merely heatmap consistency but have not been empirically verified yet.
3. **Actual safety and review constraints**, never assuming a negative object class equals true task-specific non-compliance; if task requirements are missing, occupational clearance rates are `NOT_ESTIMABLE` rather than fabricated.
4. **Measured incremental resources** with a matched selective competitor (calls, latency, memory and optional correctly metered electrical Joules), not a generic unsupported "energy-aware" assertion.
