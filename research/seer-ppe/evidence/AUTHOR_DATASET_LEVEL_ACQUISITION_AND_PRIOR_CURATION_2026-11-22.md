## Normalized academic interpretation

The dataset was assembled from multiple projects and work locations in Yemen associated with civil-society organizations. Its purpose is analytical research into PPE detection and occupational-safety conditions, with the aim of developing practical solutions. The identity of every individual worksite, project or organization is not a required analytical variable for this study and need not be published or reconstructed for every image.

The author states that image review/selection and dataset preparation were completed during the original collection and database-preparation phase. This declaration is consistent with the earlier V18 author-curator record, which accepts the author's completed image/object review as a single-curator process and explicitly rejects mandatory repeated full-corpus reinspection.

## Methodological scope

1. **Aggregate provenance is the appropriate description.** Report the corpus as a multi-project, multi-location Yemeni field-image collection associated with civil-society organization projects. Do not invent or require a project/site ID for each image when site identity is not an outcome, predictor or sampling stratum in the research question.
2. **No repeat full-corpus review.** The original author-curated selection, annotation and image review are accepted as the existing single-curator process. The present research phase does not require reopening and re-reviewing all 2,327 images or all 459 mapped Clean17 Validation images merely to populate site names.
3. **Separate source provenance from split leakage.** Dataset-level multi-source origin does not prove that every Train and Validation image is acquisition-independent. Existing exact-duplicate, near-duplicate, same-scene and other credible cross-split candidates remain subject to the already frozen technical/human overlap controls.
4. **Known overlaps remain controlled.** The two School 22 May Valid records remain hard-held due to their shared Train site family. Valid WA0026 remains hard-held because the author confirmed the same scene content as Train WA0079 with changes/processing. The other unresolved P0 candidates retain their existing quarantine/hold.
5. **No overclaim.** This statement does not establish a complete per-image chain of custody, independent site sampling, independent acquisition-family labels, an untouched Test set, independent reviewer agreement, worker-level PPE gold, or site-independent generalization.
6. **Analysis boundary.** Existing author-annotation-backed internal image-level detection analyses may proceed with exact source/label/prediction joins and transparent overlap-risk sensitivity cohorts. Claims about independent multi-site generalization or verified worker-level safety require their own evidence and remain separately gated.

## Researcher decision

For the SEER-PPE thesis, **per-image worksite/project identification is not a prerequisite**. The corpus will be described at the aggregate acquisition-context level. The completed original curation is accepted without repeating a full image review. The research will continue to screen and exclude/quarantine only credible cross-split overlap candidates already identified by the frozen audit, while reporting the absence of a complete per-image site register as a limitation where relevant.
