## Executive findings

**Strongest specialist priority if the institutional requirement accepts ESCI-based JCR Q1 and a more selective application venue:** **Visual Computing for Industry, Biomedicine, and Art (VCIBA)**. Its Springer publisher page reports 2025 Journal Impact Factor **6.7**, **ESCI** coverage and a journal-society-paid **USD 0 article-processing charge**. A secondary integrated JCR/SJR record reports **2025 JCR Q1 and 2025 SJR Q1**; the official JCR category row requires independent institutional verification before a Q1 guarantee. The industrial computer-vision remit and a November 2026 worker-pose article are on topic, but the present ten-target, eight-image XAI study is likely **underpowered** for the journal's selective expectations.

**Strongest established directly focused machine-vision venue, if JCR Q3 is acceptable:** **Machine Vision and Applications**, Springer hybrid, publisher 2025 JIF **2.0**, indexed in SCIE/Scopus in external records, secondary 2025 JCR **Q3**, SCImago **Q2**. Its aims explicitly demand a *significant experimental validation component*. Requires manuscript compression and careful target-scope framing.

**Best hybrid Q2-JCR alternatives for an institution requiring JCR Q2:** **The Visual Computer** (publisher 2025 JIF **3.4**, secondary JCR **Q2**) and **Applied Intelligence** (publisher 2025 JIF **3.5**, secondary JCR **Q2**). The Visual Computer is relevant to visual analytics/vision/AI but generally expects more visual-computing methodological contribution; Applied Intelligence expects novel AI methodology or nontrivial real-world intelligent-system advances. Subscription route has **no APC** (publisher's journal-specific publishing policies) although possible other fees must be checked.

**Two additional fully free journals:** **Visual Intelligence** (gold OA with society-covered APC, Scimago **Q1 2025**, no JCR Q1 verified; Springer page does not display a JIF as of check) and **VCIBA** above. These are **diamond open access**: no APC to any author, independent of Yemen eligibility.

**Promising accessible gold-OA backup:** **Discover Artificial Intelligence**, Springer fully OA (current stated APC **USD 1,690**, charged upon acceptance), explicitly covers applied computer vision. Springer Nature's **July 2026 policy lists Yemen for a full APC waiver** when the corresponding author is based in Yemen, subject to standard-workflow eligibility and requesting waiver **at submission**. Springer announces that the journal is indexed in **Scopus and ESCI**; secondary sources show 2025 SJR Q1; **do not advertise an unverified JCR Q1**.

**Important red flags:**
- **Signal, Image and Video Processing** has publisher-imposed **maximum 10 final two-column pages** including text/figures/references, page 10 references only. This makes the 62-page current main manuscript unsuitable without complete rewriting. Publisher 2025 JIF **2.7**, secondary 2025 JCR **Q3**, SJR **Q2**.
- **Pattern Analysis and Applications** explicitly requires **public access to experimental code and data**, an English-commented executable README, and comparison of methods under exactly identical conditions. The author-curated construction worker images may have privacy/licensing restrictions. Therefore **conditional, not first-tier**, even though publisher JIF 2025 **2.6**.
- **Journal of Real-Time Image Processing** explicitly rejects papers without measured real-time algorithm/implementation/hardware problems. The recorded V29 hosted round-trip is not an on-device latency/real-time study; unsuitable now.
- **Multimedia Tools and Applications** fits multimedia CV, but **2025 publisher homepage does not display a JIF** and Pusan National University journal metadata lists **SCIE discontinued 1996–2024** while Scopus remains active. Avoid for institutional JCR/SCIE requirements absent direct Clarivate Master Journal List confirmation. A SJR Q1 in a category does not restore SCIE accreditation.
- **International Journal of Computer Vision** 2025 JIF **10.3**, expects major broadly generalizable technical advances. Not a realistic first submission on current fixed-detector eight-image XAI evidence.
- **Neural Computing and Applications**, **International Journal of Machine Learning and Cybernetics** and **Applied Intelligence** all cover AI/ML; the first two demand substantial method novelty/stronger comparison and are lower priorities for the current bounded case study.

## Current manuscript fit and revision gates

Current main paper v0.43: 62 pages, 16 tables, 1 summary figure; separate supplement 34 pages and 10 paired XAI maps. Original-source Valid 459 images, 3,471 GT, TP=3,297, FP=806, FN=174, micro-F1=0.8706. Ten selected perturbation targets in eight development-exposed images, no primary independent-site XAI validation, no inferential superiority of A5/A6 policy, signed-map rank agreement Spearman rho=0.5416, Kendall tau-b=0.4239, top-four overlap=65%. The fixed RF-DETR architecture and earlier PPE detector/XAI conceptual framework are previously published by the author.

**Best-fit framing:** *Target-Matched Perturbation Responses and Grid-Resolution Stability in PPE Object Detection: A Frozen RF-DETR Case Study*, not a validated worker-compliance/safety-reduction system.

**Before any Springer submission:** author must document person-image ethics approval/exemption if applicable and privacy/consent/legal image rights, funding/COI/contributions, prior-work nonduplication, data/code sharing conditions; shorten and restructure to the journal's article format; move historical V-stage headings, source audits and most tables to supplements; make the 10-target XAI scope explicit and avoid generalized confidence claims. Preserve V27 chronology and V34-B PDF duplicate warnings in internal provenance records unless original evidence permits resolution. Do not claim onsite inference latency or occupational-compliance outcomes.

**If Q1 JCR is non-negotiable:** ask university explicitly whether **ESCI JCR Q1** qualifies (VCIBA is ESCI), and retrieve 2025 Clarivate JCR category rankings officially before submitting. If SCIE is independently required, VCIBA may **not** meet the rule despite its JCR Q1; consider stronger work targeting a specialist SCIE Q1 instead. If lower budget is the decisive priority, VCIBA and Visual Intelligence are diamond-OA irrespective of Yemen.
