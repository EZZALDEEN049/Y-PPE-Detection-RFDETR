# EcoSafe-RFDETR Claims Register

## Claims allowed before experiments
- The study investigates safety-constrained adaptive computation for PPE monitoring.
- Energy will be measured directly rather than inferred only from FLOPs.
- The router is designed to use uncertainty, distribution-shift evidence, and safety relevance.

## Claims prohibited before empirical validation
- EcoSafe-RFDETR reduces energy.
- EcoSafe-RFDETR preserves safety performance.
- EcoSafe-RFDETR outperforms fixed detectors.
- EcoSafe-RFDETR generalizes better under domain shift.

## Novelty wording to avoid
Do not claim: first energy-efficient PPE detector, first uncertainty-aware PPE system, first adaptive detector, or first risk-aware PPE framework.

## Defensible novelty direction
The contribution is the explicit coupling of per-input computational allocation with calibrated reliability/domain-shift evidence and PPE safety relevance, evaluated using directly measured inference energy under an explicit safety-performance constraint.
